About Us

Universe Yoga/NIHY

Universe Yoga (NIHY) is Jaipur's most trusted yoga establishment, located in the heart of Sodala, Ganesh Nagar. Founded and led by the highly respected Yoga Acharya Prabhakar Shrivastava, the center is dedicated to teaching traditional yoga with clarity, patience, and a deep focus on genuine health benefits. With a remarkable rating of 4.9 out of 5, Universe Yoga has earned the trust and admiration of students of all experience levels across the city.

At Universe Yoga, every session is designed to help students understand each asana properly and experience real, lasting improvements in physical and mental well-being. Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced practitioner, Prabhakar Shrivastava sir's calm and structured teaching style ensures that yoga becomes an accessible and transformative part of your daily routine. Join our growing community and take the first step toward a healthier, more balanced life.
